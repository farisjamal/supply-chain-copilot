# Logistics Standard Operating Procedures

## Inbound Shipment Tracking
All inbound purchase orders are tracked in the shipments table with order date, promised date, and delivered date. Status is updated daily from carrier EDI feeds.

## Late Shipment Escalation
- 1–3 days late: logistics coordinator emails the supplier for a revised ETA.
- 4–7 days late: supply chain manager is notified; expedite options are evaluated.
- 8+ days late: escalate to head of procurement; invoke contractual late-delivery penalties and assess alternate sourcing.

## Receiving and Putaway
Received goods must be inspected within 24 hours. Discrepancies (quantity or damage) are logged and reported to the supplier within 48 hours to preserve claim rights.

## Carrier Selection
Sea freight is default for replenishment orders. Air freight requires manager approval and is reserved for stockout-risk SKUs or customer-committed dates.

## Peak Season Planning
October through December is peak season. Purchase orders for peak demand must be placed by August 31. Supplier capacity reservations are confirmed in July.
