# Supplier Agreement — Acme Components Ltd (SUP-01)

## Overview
Acme Components Ltd, based in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam, supplies cables and pointing devices (SKU-1001 USB-C Cable, SKU-1002 Wireless Mouse). Contract effective January 1, 2025 through December 31, 2026.

## Payment Terms
Net 45 days from invoice date. A 2% early-payment discount applies if paid within 10 days (2/10 net 45). Invoices are issued in USD.

## Lead Time and Delivery
Standard lead time is 12 calendar days from purchase order confirmation, shipped FOB Ho Chi Minh City. Expedited air freight available at buyer's cost, reducing lead time to 5 days.

## Late Delivery Penalties
If a shipment is delivered more than 3 business days after the promised date, Acme credits 1% of the shipment value per additional late day, capped at 8% of the shipment value.

## Minimum Order Quantities
SKU-1001: 500 units. SKU-1002: 250 units. Orders below MOQ incur a 15% surcharge.

## Quality and Returns
Defect rate above 1.5% in any inspected lot entitles the buyer to reject the lot. Returns for defects are at Acme's expense, with replacement units shipped within 10 days.
