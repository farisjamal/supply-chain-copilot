# Supplier Agreement — Shenzhen Electro Co (SUP-02)

## Overview
Shenzhen Electro Co supplies keyboards and monitors (SKU-1003 Mechanical Keyboard, SKU-1004 27in Monitor). Contract effective March 1, 2025, auto-renews annually unless terminated with 90 days notice.

## Payment Terms
Net 30 days from bill of lading date. A 30% deposit is required on purchase orders above USD 50,000. Payments in USD via wire transfer.

## Lead Time and Delivery
Standard lead time is 18 calendar days, shipped FOB Shenzhen. Shenzhen Electro's historical on-time delivery rate is the lowest in our supplier base (~82%); orders for peak season should be placed at least 30 days ahead.

## Late Delivery Penalties
No contractual penalty for the first 5 late days. Beyond 5 days, a flat 3% credit of shipment value applies. This weaker penalty clause was accepted in exchange for a 7% lower unit price.

## Minimum Order Quantities
SKU-1003: 100 units. SKU-1004: 50 units.

## Escalation
Persistent lateness (3+ late shipments in a rolling quarter) triggers a mandatory quarterly business review and allows sourcing up to 40% of volume from an alternate supplier without breach.
